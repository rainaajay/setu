The committee

The ledger was reset on 12 August 2026 to clear a divergence that no longer had a repair path: one authority had fallen ~31,000 sequence numbers behind and was widening, because catch-up runs strictly in order and the certificate it needed had been evicted by every peer. Counters above therefore start from that date. Each authority keeps roughly its last two days of certificates, so a short outage heals automatically — but an authority left behind for longer than that window still cannot be repaired, and a "behind by N" figure would then rise rather than fall. See THREAT_MODEL.md.
